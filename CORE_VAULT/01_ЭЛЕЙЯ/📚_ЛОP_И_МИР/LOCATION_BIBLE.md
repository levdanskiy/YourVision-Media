# 🏙️ ELEYIA: LOCATION & INFRASTRUCTURE BIBLE (REALISM V2.0)

## 1. ELEJA (THE ACADEMIC HUB)
*   **Architecture:** Neoclassical Elven Splendor. A massive white marble palace where ancient ionic columns seamlessly house fiber-optic amber-veins. It is the spiritual and technical heart of the Lyceum.
*   **The Whispering Library:** Multi-layered mahogany shelves where physical grimoires coexist with floating AR-data. The "Whisper" is a result of advanced acoustic engineering designed to mimic the voice of the Sky Breach.
*   **The Aetherium:** The central assembly hall. A circular marble floor with a massive pulsing amber sigil that acts as a primary grounding node for the students' collective resonance.
*   **Atmosphere:**
    *   *Visual:* Sharp shadows, gray stone, glowing amber data-cables running along ceilings.
    *   *Sound:* Constant low-frequency hum of the cooling systems.
    *   *Resonance:* Neurological pressure. A sense of being monitored.

## 2. RIGA-PRIME (THE CAPITAL)
*   **Architecture:** Hyper-Capitalist Brutalism. Steel, glass, and exposed Amber-conduits. The city is a vertical power grid.
*   **The Monolith District:** Corporate headquarters built to withstand 1974-level resonance spikes. Surfaces are covered in adaptive shielding.
*   **The Low-Sector Market:** Grit and high-tech junk. Illicit trade of repurposed Suppressors and raw Amber scraps.
*   **Atmosphere:**
    *   *Visual:* Harsh neon, wet pavement, massive cooling towers venting blue steam.
    *   *Sound:* The shriek of MAGLEV brakes; the buzz of faulty billboards.
    *   *Resonance:* Mental exhaustion. Constant sensory bombardment.

## 3. KONIGSBERG (THE RESEARCH CORE)
*   **Architecture:** Cold Minimalism (CERN-vibe). Smooth concrete, polarized windows, and geometric silence. No ornamentation.
*   **Atmosphere:**
    *   *Visual:* Endless white corridors; scientists in grey lead-lined coats.
    *   *Sound:* Total silence enforced by acoustic dampeners.
    *   *Resonance:* Emotional void. The Suppressors here are at maximum power.
