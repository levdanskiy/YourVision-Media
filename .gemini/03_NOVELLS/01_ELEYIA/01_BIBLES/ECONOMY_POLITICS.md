# 💰 ELEYIA: ECONOMY & POWER STRUCTURE (V1.0)

## 1. THE PULSE CURRENCY (DENOMINATIONS)
*   **Milliamps (mA):** Daily micro-transactions.
*   **Watts (W):** Standard unit of value. 1 Watt = 1 hour of life-support.
*   **Kilowatts (kW):** High-end asset transfers. Luxury tier.
*   **Transactions:** Performed via "Resonance Bump" (bracelet contact). Fully tracked by the Ministry.
*   **The Dust Market:** Illegal trade of raw Amber shards. Unstable, anonymous, dangerous.

## 2. INSTITUTIONAL POWER
*   **The Consensus of Nine:** Global supreme council. They regulate the planetary resonance frequency.
*   **The Ministry of Resonance:** The executive body. Responsible for Edict enforcement, Suppressor maintenance, and energy taxation.
*   **Lord Oeron's Status:** *High Arbiter of Hansa Energy*. He controls the infrastructure of the Baltic resonance grid.

## 3. SOCIAL STRATIFICATION
*   **Sovereigns:** Pure Elven lineages & Human oligarchs. Unlimited Pulse access.
*   **Contributors:** Registered Splits. The biological batteries of the world. High taxation, low mobility.
*   **Zero-Class:** Non-magic humans. Consumers only. Zero-worth in the energy market.

## 4. REVE VISUALS OF POWER
*   **Wealth Indicators:** "Bright, steady glow of the Pulse-bracelet", "Liquid Silk garments with high transparency", "exclusive access to natural sunlight (rare in Riga-Prime)".
*   **Poverty Indicators:** "Flickering or dim bracelets", "Heavy lead-lined grey rags", "Deep shadows and cold environments".
