*   **MAGLEV Racing:** High-speed racing where pilots sync their Pulse with vehicle engines for 100% efficiency.
*   **Aura-Concerts:** Sensory-direct performances. Music experienced as pure emotional and physical vibration.

## 3. CALIBRATION & MEDICINE
*   **Resonance Calibration:** Medical reset of a Split's baseline frequency to remove "Emotional Residue".

## 4. THE SHADOW SECTOR (CRIME)

*   **The Hollows:** Lawless, unlit districts with zero energy grid access.
