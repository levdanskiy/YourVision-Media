# 🌍 GLOBAL GEOPOLITICS: THE AMBER CONSENSUS (V1.0)

## 1. THE NINE RESONANCE BLOCKS
*   **Amber Alliance (Hansa 2.0):** The Hub of Purity. Focus: Energy exports & Suppression Laws. Center: Riga-Prime.
*   **USA Totem:** Tech-Shamanism. Focus: AI-Spirits & Digital Totems.
*   **Sino-Dragon Empire:** Bio-Engineering. Focus: Organic Tech & Living Cities.
*   **Sacred Roman Directorate:** Vampire Hegemony. Focus: Blood Ledgers & Heritage Banking.
*   **Slavic Veche:** Earth Magic. Focus: Natural Resonance & Raw Power.
*   **Ifrit Sultanates:** Solar Alchemy. Focus: Thermal Amber & Desert Luxury.
*   **Bharat Matrix:** Neural Coding. Focus: Cognitive Networks & Fractal Logic.
*   **Southern Cross Union:** Deep Sea Veins. Focus: Abyssal Amber & Marine Stability.
*   **Andean Federation:** Botanical Alchemy. Focus: Bio-Medicine & Empathetic Healing.

## 2. THE PULSE ECONOMY
*   **Currency:** The Pulse (KW/h per heartbeat).
*   **Energy Taxation:** The Alliance harvests 40% of every Split's ambient resonance via the Suppressor Network.
*   **The Dark Zones:** Regions or social classes with zero Pulse access. 

## 3. THE SKY BREACH PHENOMENON
*   A global atmospheric rupture.
*   **The Great Shift:** The Breach changes color based on the region's dominant frequency (e.g., Turquoise in Hansa, Red in Veche).
*   **Navigation:** Commercial flights are impossible without "Amber-Shielding".

## 4. GLOBAL THREATS
*   **Resonance Overload:** A theoretical event where the Sky Breach collapses, wiping out all electronic and magical life.
*   **The Pure-Blood Purge:** A secret political movement aimed at removing Elven traits from humanity.
