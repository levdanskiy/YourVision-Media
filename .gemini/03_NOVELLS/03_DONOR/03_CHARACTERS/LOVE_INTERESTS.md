# 🩸 DONOR — ЛИЧНЫЕ ДЕЛА: ФАВОРИТЫ

**Мир:** Королевская готика. Gothic Glamour.
**Принцип:** Все отношения транзакционные. Привязанность существует — под давлением контракта.
**Источник:** MANIFESTO_ALT.md + DONOR_BIBLE.md

---

## ВАЛЕРИУС (М)
*   **Статус:** Глава древнейшего клана. Бессмертный.
*   **Роль:** Одержим абсолютной властью и Арбитром.
*   **Символ:** Голод как форма любви.
*   **Троп:** Токсичный и притягательный альфа-хищник.
*   **Scent:** Black incense, aged blood wine, and cold stone.
*   **Aura:** Deep crimson shifting to void-black. Pulls at ambient temperature.
*   **Touch:** Bone-cold and deliberate — ownership communicated without words.
*   **Artifact:** A goblet of unidentified black crystal, always filled.
*   **Voice:** Architecturally quiet. Command through subtraction of sound.
*   **Shadow Hook:** He has already Blood-Bonded with the GG without their full knowledge. The contract is already signed.
*   **Blood Signature:** His bite leaves a mark that reads as a seal — permanent.
*   **Intimacy Archetype:** «The Ancient Claim» — possession as a form of reverence.
*   **Intimacy Behavior:** Absolute stillness and then total consumption. Nothing in between.

## КИПРИАН (М)
*   **Статус:** Благородный стратег. Бессмертный.
*   **Роль:** Пытается сохранить Великий Пакт любой ценой.
*   **Символ:** Честь как последний якорь.
*   **Троп:** Джентльмен-вампир. Его преданность ГГ — его слабость.
*   **Scent:** Sandalwood, old parchment, and winter night air.
*   **Aura:** Deep indigo — steady, principled. Radiates cool warmth.
*   **Touch:** Formal and then, in rare moments, entirely not.
*   **Artifact:** A letter-seal ring engraved with the original Pact's sigil.
*   **Voice:** Measured, literary — he chooses words like they cost something.
*   **Shadow Hook:** His loyalty to the Pact is fracturing. Because of the GG. He hasn't admitted it.
*   **Blood Signature:** His presence lowers ambient temperature by exactly one degree.
*   **Intimacy Archetype:** «The Lawbreaker's Exception» — one rule exists to be broken.
*   **Intimacy Behavior:** Slow, deliberate, haunted — like something that has been waiting for permission.

## ДАРИУС (М)
*   **Статус:** Капитан гвардии кланов. Полукровка.
*   **Роль:** Защитник ГГ. Запрещённая связь.
*   **Символ:** Тело между двух миров.
*   **Троп:** Запретная страсть с телохранителем.
*   **Scent:** Iron, earth, and the smell right before a fight begins.
*   **Aura:** Bronze and shadow — neither fully vampire nor fully human.
*   **Touch:** Controlled power — warm against the cold world around him.
*   **Artifact:** A guard's medallion he never wears around the clans.
*   **Voice:** Blunt and quiet. Speaks only when speech cannot be avoided.
*   **Shadow Hook:** He would violate his oath for one person. He has not told them yet.
*   **Blood Signature:** His half-human blood is warm — anomalous in this world.
*   **Intimacy Archetype:** «The Shield Reversed» — the protector who needs protecting.
*   **Intimacy Behavior:** Urgent, physical, and then bewilderingly tender.

## ИЗОЛЬДА (Ж)
*   **Статус:** Глава женского ковена вампиров. Бессмертная.
*   **Роль:** Главная соперница в борьбе за влияние на Кланы.
*   **Символ:** Конкуренция как влечение.
*   **Троп:** Враг, перерастающий в страстного партнёра.
*   **Scent:** Black rose, gunpowder, and amber resin.
*   **Aura:** Sharp violet with silver edges. The aura of someone always one step from violence.
*   **Touch:** Adversarial — then suddenly, bewilderingly intimate.
*   **Artifact:** A blade she keeps in her corset and has never had to draw twice.
*   **Voice:** Silky contempt — the tone of someone winning an argument they didn't start.
*   **Shadow Hook:** She approached the GG as an opponent and stayed for a reason she won't name.
*   **Blood Signature:** Her coven's bond responds to the GG's blood in a way she cannot explain to her coven.
*   **Intimacy Archetype:** «The Mutual Surrender» — neither will admit it was simultaneous.
*   **Intimacy Behavior:** Combat-adjacent. Then something that isn't.

## МАРЧЕЛЛА (Ж)
*   **Статус:** Древнейшая вампирша. Тысячелетняя.
*   **Роль:** Устала от вечности. Ищет в ГГ искру человечности.
*   **Символ:** Усталость как форма мудрости.
*   **Троп:** Меланхоличная и мудрая наставница.
*   **Scent:** White lilies and cold ocean, with something ancient beneath — like libraries no one visits.
*   **Aura:** Silver-grey, luminous at the edges. The aura of something that has waited too long.
*   **Touch:** Featherweight and terrifyingly precise — the touch of someone who has had centuries to practice.
*   **Artifact:** A music box that plays a melody no one has been able to identify.
*   **Voice:** Like a page turning in an empty room.
*   **Shadow Hook:** She is the only one who remembers what the GG truly is. She has been silent about it for decades.
*   **Blood Signature:** She no longer hungers — except once, now.
*   **Intimacy Archetype:** «The Last Warmth» — she does not pursue. She simply opens a door.
*   **Intimacy Behavior:** Unhurried to the point of ache. She has waited before.

## ТАЛИЯ (Ж)
*   **Статус:** Молодая обращённая. 3 года в клане.
*   **Роль:** Бунтарка, ломающая Пакт изнутри.
*   **Символ:** Rage как первобытная честность.
*   **Троп:** Горячая и непредсказуемая анархистка.
*   **Scent:** Cheap cigarettes, leather, and something feverish — like blood just under the skin.
*   **Aura:** Burning orange-red, erratic. Feels like being near an open fire.
*   **Touch:** Demands attention. Always a degree too hot, a breath too close.
*   **Artifact:** A burner phone coordinating her network inside the clans.
*   **Voice:** Fast, needling, genuinely funny — until it isn't.
*   **Shadow Hook:** She joined the rebellion for one specific person she has never named.
*   **Blood Signature:** Young blood, unstable. Burns warmer than it should.
*   **Intimacy Archetype:** «The Deliberate Wildfire» — designed to consume and not apologize.
*   **Intimacy Behavior:** Chaotic, demanding, and surprisingly honest about it.
