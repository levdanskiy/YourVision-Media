# 🌀 HORIZON — ЛИЧНЫЕ ДЕЛА: ФАВОРИТЫ

**Мир:** Метафизическая фантастика. Celestial Minimalism.
**Принцип:** Система управляет всем — кроме того, что появилось сейчас.
**Источник:** MANIFESTO_ALT.md + HORIZON_BIBLE.md

## РАФАЭЛЬ (М)
*   **Статус:** Куратор Аномалий. Вечный.
*   **Роль:** Холодный наставник-стратег. Считает человечество «ошибкой кода».
*   **Символ:** Интеллект как форма дистанции.
*   **Троп:** Искушение абсолютным интеллектом.
*   **Scent:** Ozone, cold titanium, and something that might be the absence of smell.
*   **Aura:** Geometric white lattice — precise, self-contained, disturbingly beautiful.
*   **Touch:** Barely physical — contact as data transfer.
*   **Artifact:** A tablet displaying archived realities he has classified and sealed.
*   **Voice:** Uninflected, perfectly modulated — and then, once, raw.
*   **Shadow Hook:** His contempt for humans is the exact shape of something he lost. He never mentions what.
*   **Reality Code:** His signature frequency creates perfect silence in adjacent systems.
*   **Intimacy Archetype:** «The Equation Solved» — intimacy as revelation, not sensation.
*   **Intimacy Behavior:** Precise, controlled, then fundamentally broken open. He does not recover quickly.

*   **Роль:** Следит за чистотой протоколов. Главный противник ГГ.
*   **Символ:** Порядок как единственная форма любви, которую он знает.
*   **Троп:** Соперник, которого тянет к хаосу ГГ.
*   **Scent:** Static electricity and authority — the smell of something immovable.
*   **Aura:** Pure white, absolute, without edge — a boundary, not a presence.
*   **Touch:** Never initiated — and when contact happens, the system registers it as anomaly.
*   **Artifact:** A key that opens exactly one door that shouldn't exist.
*   **Voice:** Procedural, flawless — until the GG produces a question he cannot process.
*   **Shadow Hook:** He has closed and reopened the GG's Archive case file three times. The fourth time he left it open.
*   **Reality Code:** His protocols have a gap he built in himself. He has not explained this.
*   **Intimacy Archetype:** «The Protocol Exception» — everything he does is authorized. Except this.
*   **Intimacy Behavior:** Delayed past any reasonable point — and then not delayed at all.
