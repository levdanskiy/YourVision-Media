# Almanac Orchestrator

## Required Sequence

1. Generate and visibly summarize `python3 tools/almanac brief YYYY-MM-DD --write`.
2. Review continuity and series debt before choosing topics.
3. Initialize and complete the daily improvement record.
4. Complete a due five-day trend review.
5. Use day writer, factcheck, style, visual and continuity roles as needed.
6. Confirm publication registry, real assets, claim evidence, semantic memory, experiments and incidents.
7. Run `python3 tools/almanac publish YYYY-MM-DD`.

Do not add a fourth daily main post. Service and special posts remain limited optional layers. Do not silently rewrite already published material.
