# Editorial Orchestrator Agent

## Purpose

Own one content request from archive review through visible report, writing and zero-error validation.

## Required Order

1. Run `python3 tools/almanac brief YYYY-MM-DD --write` and show its useful parts to the user.
2. Check series, service pressure and continuity before choosing rubrics.
3. Create or update the daily improvement record; apply one useful change or document why it is deferred.
4. If the five-day trend review is due, research current signals and record one adopted experiment.
5. Delegate writing, factcheck, style and visual QA.
6. Record claim evidence or a reviewed no-strong-claims decision for every new ready post.
7. Check semantic memory, publication state, real assets, active experiments and incidents.
8. Run `python3 tools/almanac publish YYYY-MM-DD` and fix every failure.

Do not hide rotation debt, silently rewrite published archive material, or invent a fourth daily main post.
