# FOOD TECHNOLOGY MATRIX

Use this as a planning map for 15:04. It is not a source of factual claims.
Before publication, verify each dish, technique, date, and safety detail through
`SOURCE_DATABASES.md` and `FACTCHECK_RULES.md`.

## Heat And Transformation

| Mechanism | Host Rubrics | Use For |
|-----------|--------------|---------|
| Open fire / radiant heat | `FOODWAYS`, `TOOLS`, `PIES`, `FLATBREAD` | Grill, hearth, barbecue, tandyr/tandoor, brazier, smoking fire. |
| Closed dry heat | `CAKES`, `BUNS`, `PATISSERIE`, `BREAD`, `PIES` | Baking, roasting, oven architecture, crust formation. |
| Contact surface | `FLATBREAD`, `TOOLS`, `RECIPE`, `CONDIMENTS` | Comal, tava, saj, wok, pan, caramel surface, griddle. |
| Liquid medium | `SOUP`, `RECIPE`, `DESSERTS`, `FOODWAYS` | Soup, broth, porridge, custard, poaching, blanching. |
| Fat as medium | `SWEETS`, `PIES`, `FOODWAYS`, `RECIPE` | Fritters, doughnuts, tempura, falafel, churros, samosa frying. |
| Steam | `FOODWAYS`, `TOOLS`, `RECIPE`, `DESSERTS` | Dumplings, manty, dim sum, idli, couscous, steamed pudding. |
| No heat | `FOODWAYS`, `PRESERVE`, `CONDIMENTS` | Ceviche, tartare, cured fish, vinegar denaturation, raw service rules. |
| Living culture | `FERMENT` | Sourdough, kimchi, miso, kefir, kvass, kombucha, injera, dosa batter. |
| Salt/smoke/air/sugar | `PRESERVE` | Curing, drying, smoking, jam, confit, pickles, syrups, salt-preserved citrus. |
| Sugar stage | `SWEETS`, `RECIPE`, `PATISSERIE` | Syrup stages, caramel, brittle, praline, nougat, fudge, crystallization. |
| Emulsion / paste | `CONDIMENTS`, `RECIPE` | Tahini, aioli, pesto, harissa, chutney, mustard, fermented sauces. |

## Food Format Gaps To Keep Covered

| Format | Default Rubric | Lenses |
|--------|----------------|--------|
| Soup / broth / cold soup | `SOUP` | `FASTING_FOOD`, `SACRED_FOOD`, `STAPLE` |
| Grain bowl / porridge / staple grain | `FOODWAYS` or `SOUP` | `STAPLE`, `FOOD_POWER` |
| Fermented food or drink as process | `FERMENT` | `SACRED_FOOD`, `FOOD_ROUTE` |
| Preserved/cured/smoked food | `PRESERVE` | `FOOD_POWER`, `MARKET` |
| Sauce / paste / condiment | `CONDIMENTS` | `FOOD_ROUTE`, `MARKET`, `SACRED_FOOD` |
| Street food | `FOODWAYS` | `STREET`, sometimes `PIES` / `FLATBREAD` |
| Fasting food | `FOODWAYS`, `SOUP`, `FEAST` | `FASTING_FOOD`, `FAST` |
| Sacred or purity-coded food | `FOODWAYS`, `FEAST` | `SACRED_FOOD`, `TABOO` |
| Food and power/class | `FOODWAYS`, `PANTRY`, `CONDIMENTS` | `FOOD_POWER` |
| Ingredient route | `FOODWAYS`, `DRINKS`, `CONDIMENTS`, `PANTRY` | `FOOD_ROUTE` |

## Monthly Minimums

Every full month after July ramp-up should include:
- at least 1 living-culture process (`FERMENT`);
- at least 1 salt/smoke/sugar/acid preservation post (`PRESERVE`);
- at least 1 soup/liquid food post (`SOUP`);
- at least 1 condiment/sauce/paste post (`CONDIMENTS`);
- at least 1 food-and-power or food-route lens;
- at least 1 non-European staple or street-food lens.
