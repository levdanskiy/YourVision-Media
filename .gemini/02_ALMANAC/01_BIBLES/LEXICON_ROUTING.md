# LEXICON ROUTING

Effective from `2026-07-23`.

`ALMANAC: TABLE` is recipe-only. All standalone non-recipe material moves to:

- Channel: https://t.me/lexicon_by_AlmanacMarg
- Project: `/home/levdanskiy/.gemini/05_LEXICON`

## Almanac Keeps

- reproducible recipes;
- food techniques;
- table/menu/shopping/prep service notes;
- myth, calendar, source, divination, etymology and ritual only as short recipe context.

## LEXICON Receives

| Material | LEXICON Slot |

| Omens, superstitions, tarot, astrology, zodiac, I Ching, runes, forecast, fragments | `08:44 AURORA` |
| Personae, bestiary, deities, spirits, folk roles, heritage | `11:27 ANIMA` |

## Border Rule

If the post contains ingredient quantities and a reproducible preparation method,
it belongs to Almanac.

If the post is about symbol, text, date, omen, figure, object, place or ritual
without a recipe, it belongs to LEXICON.

If both are possible, choose the object:

- bread of Lammas as recipe -> Almanac;
- Lammas as calendar/etymology -> LEXICON;
- tarot week forecast -> LEXICON;
- recipe menu for a week with calendar dates -> Almanac `MENU-RADAR`;
- mythic creature as dossier -> LEXICON;
- food named after a creature with full method -> Almanac.
