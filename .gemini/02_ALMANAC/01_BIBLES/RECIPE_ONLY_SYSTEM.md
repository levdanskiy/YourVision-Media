# RECIPE ONLY SYSTEM

Effective from `2026-07-23`.

From this date Almanac becomes a recipe-led channel. The old three-theme
architecture remains archive/corpus knowledge, but it no longer owns daily
`AL` slots.

## Core Decision

- Daily main rhythm stays at exactly 3 `AL` posts.
- Main slots stay equally spaced: `09:04`, `15:04`, `21:04`.
- Every `AL` post from `2026-07-23` is a reproducible recipe.
- Every `AL` post contains `ИНГРЕДИЕНТЫ` and `ПРИГОТОВЛЕНИЕ`.
- Myth, folklore, calendar, divination, language and history are allowed only
  as a recipe headnote, source note, serving context or cultural layer.
- No standalone `LORE`, `SOURCE`, `CALENDAR`, `CHRONOS`, `DIVINATION`,
  `ETYMON`, `PROSE`, `PERSONAE`, `BESTIARY`, `OBJECTS`, `RITES`, `MODERN`,
  `CYCLES`, `WHEEL`, `HERITAGE`, `FEAST` as daily `AL` after `2026-07-22`.

This is not a softer food channel. It is a global recipe archive with stronger
utility, source discipline and editorial visuals.

## Slot Logic

| Slot | Name | Reader Job | Best Rubrics |
|------|------|------------|--------------|
| `09:04` | Prep / Base | recipe to start, soak, mix, ferment, shop for or save | `FERMENT`, `PANTRY`, `DRINKS`, `CONDIMENTS`, `FLATBREAD`, `BREAD`, `WORKFLOW`, `RECIPE` |
| `15:04` | Cook / Table | main daily cooking, savory format, street/table recipe | `SOUP`, `PIES`, `FLATBREAD`, `BREAD`, `FOODWAYS`, `CONDIMENTS`, `PRESERVE`, `RECIPE` |
| `21:04` | Slow / Sweet / Tomorrow | dessert, baking, preserves, next-day prep, feast recipe | `CAKES`, `BUNS`, `PATISSERIE`, `DESSERTS`, `SWEETS`, `PRESERVE`, `FERMENT`, `BREAD`, `RECIPE` |

Slots are recipe moods, not meal-time prison. A soup can appear at `21:04` if
it is a next-day broth; a cake can appear at `09:04` if the post is about
starter, soaking, chilling or mise en place.

## Allowed Daily Rubrics

Use only these filename rubrics for `AL` after `2026-07-23`:

`CAKES`, `BUNS`, `PATISSERIE`, `DESSERTS`, `SWEETS`, `BREAD`, `FLATBREAD`,
`PIES`, `FERMENT`, `PRESERVE`, `SOUP`, `CONDIMENTS`, `MAINS`, `SALADS`,
`GRAINS`, `LEGUMES`, `VEGETABLES`, `SEAFOOD`, `NOODLES`, `RICE`, `SPICES`,
`STREET FOOD`, `STEW`, `CURRY`, `GRILL`, `SMOKING`,
`BREAKFAST`, `RECIPE`, `FOODWAYS`, `PANTRY`, `DRINKS`,
`TOOLS`, `WORKFLOW`, `LISTS`, `FEAST_TABLE`.

Rules:
- `FOODWAYS`, `PANTRY`, `TOOLS`, `WORKFLOW`, `LISTS` must still produce a
  usable recipe or protocol with quantities and steps.
- `TOOLS` is allowed only when the tool changes the recipe and the reader can
  cook from the post.
- `LISTS` is allowed only as a recipe set: 3-5 mini-recipes or a recipe
  decision tree, not a general list.

New everyday recipe rubrics:
- `MAINS` - full savory dish or plate center: fish, meat, poultry, tofu, eggs,
  vegetables, grains or mixed dish. Do not split by animal/protein.
- `SALADS` - assembled raw/cooked salads where dressing, cut and timing matter.
- `GRAINS` - rice, millet, buckwheat, barley, maize, couscous, porridge, pilaf,
  risotto-like and grain-bowl recipes.
- `LEGUMES` - beans, lentils, peas, chickpeas, dal, soy and pulse-based recipes.
- `VEGETABLES` - vegetable-led recipes where the vegetable is the main object,
  not garnish.
- `BREAKFAST` - morning recipes when time, batch prep or first-meal ritual is
  the main editorial reason.

## Weekly Rotation

A full 7-day block has 21 recipes.

Minimum weekly coverage:
- 4-6 sweet recipes: `CAKES/BUNS/PATISSERIE/DESSERTS/SWEETS`.
- 4-6 savory baking or staple recipes: `BREAD/FLATBREAD/PIES`.
- 6-9 broad cooking recipes: `SOUP/CONDIMENTS/FERMENT/PRESERVE/MAINS/SALADS/GRAINS/LEGUMES/VEGETABLES/BREAKFAST/DRINKS/PANTRY/FOODWAYS/RECIPE/WORKFLOW`.
- At least 1 `FERMENT` or living-culture recipe.
- At least 1 `PRESERVE` or storage recipe.
- At least 1 `SOUP` or broth/liquid meal.
- At least 1 `CONDIMENTS` sauce/paste/seasoning.
- At least 2 everyday cooking recipes from `MAINS/SALADS/GRAINS/LEGUMES/VEGETABLES/BREAKFAST`.
- At least 1 quick or low-equipment recipe.
- At least 1 slow or overnight recipe.
- At least 5 cultural zones per week; at least 10 per month.

Avoid:
- three sweet posts in one day;
- two same-rubric posts in consecutive slots;
- a whole week of oven-only recipes;
- making one country/region the default voice.

## Recipe Anatomy

Every post must contain:

1. Hook - one concrete food/process image.
2. Why today - season, date, temperature, ingredient window, planning reason.
3. Source or cultural note - short, named, factual.
4. `ИНГРЕДИЕНТЫ` - quantities, yield, optional substitutions if useful.
5. `ПРИГОТОВЛЕНИЕ` - numbered steps with time, temperature and sensory stop.
6. Failure point - what commonly breaks and how to notice it.
7. Serving/storage - how the recipe lives after cooking.
8. Kicker - one short, screenshotable line.
9. Visual prompt - process or finished object as editorial evidence.

History is not removed. It becomes the headnote and hidden layer of the recipe.

## Length

Default body target: `1900-2500` characters.

Use:
- `compact` 1200-1700 for sauces, drinks, small pantry and quick protocols.
- `standard` 1800-2300 for most recipes.
- `deep recipe` 2300-2700 for complex bakes, ferments, multi-stage desserts
  and technical `RECIPE` posts.

Do not exceed Telegram caption safety: `2600-2700` body characters.

## Visual System

Recipe-only visuals must show evidence:
- cut, crumb, steam, sauce texture, brine, bubble, crust, knife path, spoon
  drag, storage jar, failed edge, cooling rack, hand-scale object.
- no restaurant luxury styling;
- no generic flat lay;
- no decorative fantasy;
- no readable text or logos.

The prompt should answer: what does the recipe need the reader to inspect?

## Service Layer After 23.07

Standalone non-recipe service formats leave Almanac and move to LEXICON.

Allowed `SV` after `2026-07-23`:
- `MENU-RADAR` - week-ahead food dates, seasonal windows and recipe routes.
- `SHOPPING-LIST` - compact ingredient prep for the next 3-5 recipes.
- `TECHNIQUE-POLL` - one specific recipe failure or preference.
- `RECIPE-INDEX` - route through published recipes by need: cold, oven-free,
  fermented, sweet, savory, fast.
- `PREP-NOTE` - one short mise en place or storage note.

Moved to LEXICON as standalone:
- `ORACLE-NOTE`;
- `CALENDAR-RADAR`;
- general `DIRECTION-PULSE`;
- non-food `RECAP`;
- non-food `QUIZ`.

If a calendar or oracle idea is needed, it must become a recipe route:
`MENU-RADAR`, `FEAST-RECIPE`, or a food headnote inside `AL`.
