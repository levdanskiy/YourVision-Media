## Август: Новая Сетка

Август не делится на старые блоки `выпечка / мифы / праздники`. Он делится по слотам.

### 09:04 - Mind / Myth / Dossier

Обязательные ветки:
- `#PERSONAE`: герои как реальные фигуры культурной памяти, dossier-формат.
- `#BESTIARY`: существа как полевые дела, следы, запреты, изображения, не fantasy-лоры.
- `#OBJECTS`: предметы мифа/ритуала как музейное или бытовое evidence.
- `#LORE/#SOURCE`: мифы, сказки, легенды, тексты, но через источник и версию.
- `#OMENS/#DIVINATION`: суеверия, гадательные формы, календарные вопросы; в `AL` без личных прогнозов.
- `#PROSE/#ETYMON`: только в утреннем или вечернем слоте, не в `15:04`.

Августовские направления:
- Деметра и первая жатва: не «лето = плодородие», а засуха/потеря/возврат.
- Первая жатва в разных системах: Lugh, Tailtiu, Demeter, grain mothers, field spirits.
- Bestiary августа: field spirits, grain wolves, harvest beings, thunder creatures, late-summer insects as omen.
- Objects августа: sickle, first sheaf, threshing floor, bread stamp, field boundary, apple in myth.
- Texts: harvest songs, almanac fragments, food words, travel prose around late summer.

### 15:04 - Food Only

Август усиливает еду шире выпечки:
- `#BREAD`: Lammas loaf, Uzbek non, Armenian matnakash, Iranian sangak/barbari, Georgian khachapuri, daily breads.
- `#FLATBREAD/#PIES`: pizza napoletana, samosa, Cornish pasty, spanakopita follow-up, empanada variants.
- `#DESSERTS/#SWEETS`: gulab jamun, dorayaki, halva, fruit compotes, frozen desserts.
- `#FERMENT`: sourdough process, kvass, kimchi, miso, kefir, tepache, pickled grains, living cultures.
- `#PRESERVE`: jam, drying, salt, vinegar, smoke, honey, oil, cold storage.
- `#SOUP`: cold soups, fasting soups, harvest broths, bean/grain soups, market soups.
- `#CONDIMENTS`: harissa, tahini, miso paste, fish sauce, adjika, chutney, herb sauces.
- `#DRINKS`: barley tea, kvass as drink when drinking culture is central, ayran/doogh, fruit shrubs, late-summer herbal infusions.
- `#FOODWAYS`: first harvest table, fasting/breaking fast, market fruit, communal baking.
- `#PANTRY`: stored staples, honey, seed storage, jars and shelves when storage is central rather than transformation.
- `#TOOLS`: tawa, tandyr/tandoor, saj, bread stamp, sieve, jam thermometer.
- `#RECIPE/#WORKFLOW`: pectin/acid/sugar, overripe fruit protocol, dough hydration in heat.

Food lenses:
- `TABLE`: meal order, shared plate, serving rules, refusal, silence, public table.
- `STREET`: stall, wrapper, queue, hand, market heat.
- `STAPLE`: rice, maize, teff, millet, cassava, oil as cultural axis.
- `FASTING_FOOD`: restriction as culinary engine.
- `FOOD_ROUTE`: trade, colonization, diaspora, port-city movement of ingredients.

Sweet and non-sweet must stay separated:
- sweet: cakes, buns, patisserie, desserts, sweets;
- non-sweet baking: bread, flatbread, pies, savory pastries;
- broad food: ferment, preserve, soup, condiments, drinks, pantry, tools, foodways.

August food coverage must include at least:
- West Asia or Central Asia;
- South Asia or Southeast Asia;
- Oceania / Island Cultures or African Diaspora;
- Global Modern food system or food science.

### 21:04 - Time / Ritual / Feast

Обязательные ветки:
- `#WHEEL/#CALENDAR`: Lammas, Lughnasadh, Спас, harvest calendars, Mabon lead-in.
- `#FEAST`: праздник через стол: хлеб, мед, мак, яблоки, first-fruits, offerings, fasting/breaking fast.
- `#HERITAGE`: living/vanishing practice with source discipline.
- `#CYCLES`: Japanese 72 ko, lunar drift, agricultural microseasons.
- `#MODERN`: modern rituals and civic/media calendars.
- `#RITES`: wedding season, school-year threshold, first harvest public rituals.
- `#CHRONOS`: light loss, dew, first cool morning, seed/dry-mass phase.

Ritual lenses:
- `GESTURE`: body micro-rituals when they belong to rites or calendar thresholds.
- `FEAST_TABLE`: table as ritual map, not just food list.
- `LOST`: use only when a calendar/practice survives as fragment, archive, or broken transmission.

Августовский тон: не «осень пришла», а «лето начинает отдавать воду, свет и плод».
