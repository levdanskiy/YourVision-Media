# DISTRIBUTION RULES

Distribution should extend Almanac without changing its editorial voice.

## Core Rule

The Telegram post remains the source. External formats are derivatives:
- quote card;
- story;
- short video/circle;
- audio reading;
- cross-post excerpt;
- long-form expansion.

Never write the original post as platform bait.

## Telegram Stories

Use `STORY_LAYER_SYSTEM.md` for templates and cadence.

Use stories as a light surface, not as a second feed.

Allowed:
- one object image before a major post;
- one poll for `SV` service questions;
- one quote/kicker from a published post;
- one "route this week" navigation story.
- one proof-of-work receipt when a source or object needs visual trust;
- one process frame for food texture or sound.

Avoid:
- daily story obligation;
- "new post, go read" spam;
- generic countdowns;
- ranking cultures, dishes, or beliefs.

## Monthly Album

Use `MONTHLY_ALBUM_SYSTEM.md`.

The monthly album is a derivative archive, not a new posting obligation.
It may become:
- PDF;
- carousel;
- Telegram media album;
- long-form archive page;
- `SV MONTHLY-ALBUM` release note.

Do not build it from popularity alone. It must preserve global coverage,
source receipts, food processes, calendar thresholds, and at least one quiet
post that matters editorially.

## Cross-Posting

Cross-post only when the post has a strong standalone object:
- source quote;
- food technique;
- calendar date;
- object/dossier image;
- one precise myth mechanism.

Format:

```text
[Kicker or source line]

[2-4 sentence excerpt]

Full post: [Telegram link]
```

Do not remove source context from excerpt if the claim is surprising.

## Audio / Voice

Audio is an optional service layer, not a new daily AL rubric.

Use `SV AUDIO-NOTE` at most once every two weeks:
- 60-90 seconds;
- one source quote, one object, one final image;
- no improvisational facts;
- no personal prediction, medical, legal, or financial advice.

Best uses:
- `SOURCE` reading;
- `PROSE` fragment;
- `ETYMON` word drift;
- `CHRONOS` seasonal field note.

## Short Video

Use video only when motion proves something:
- dough tension;
- syrup thread;
- bread crackle;
- rain on dry soil;
- page/object handling;
- table assembly.

No talking-head obligation. No decorative b-roll.

## Collaborations

Use `SV SOURCE-NOTE` or a normal AL post with clear source metadata.

Rules:
- named expert only when expertise is relevant;
- no guest authority replacing sources;
- no "culture explained by outsider" framing;
- no paid or partner content without explicit label.

## Monetization Boundary

If monetization starts, create a separate `MONETIZATION_POLICY.md` before accepting paid placement.

Until then:
- no unlabeled ads;
- no affiliate links inside AL posts;
- no sponsored myth/food/calendar claims;
- no paywalling corrections.
