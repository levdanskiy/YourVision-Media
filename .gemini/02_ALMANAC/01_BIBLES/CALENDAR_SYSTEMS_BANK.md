# CALENDAR SYSTEMS BANK

Use this as a coverage bank for `CYCLES`, `WHEEL`, `CALENDAR`, `MODERN`,
`RITES`, and `FEAST`. It is not a source of factual claims. Verify dates,
names, living/closed status, and citation rules through `SOURCE_DATABASES.md`
before writing.

|-----------|--------------|-----|

## Monthly Minimums

Every full month should include:
- at least 1 non-Gregorian living calendar;
- at least 1 historical or invented calendar;
- at least 1 fast/abstinence structure or breaking-fast table;
- at least 1 modern invented holiday with named inventor/institution/media origin;
- at least 1 commemoration or memory practice, handled in restrained register;
- at least 1 microseason/time-counting system outside the familiar solar year.
