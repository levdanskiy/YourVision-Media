### Medium - One Date, Three Independent Lenses - ADOPTED

Connect one day's three main posts with a shared `series_id`, one controlled visual motif and three different rubrics. Each post must still work alone and use a summary-safe opening.

- Experiment: `EXP-2026-07-22-ONE-DATE-THREE-LENSES`
- Hypothesis: a coherent day increases return to 15:04 and 21:04 without reducing single-post clarity.
- Metric: second- and third-slot view ratio plus forwards/saves per post.
- Review: 2026-08-05.

### Large - Five-Day Story Field File

Build a Story Album with five approved visual frames and links back to the strongest posts. Deferred until real assets and Story analytics are available.
