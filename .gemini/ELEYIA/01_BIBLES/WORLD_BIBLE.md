# 🪔 ELEYIA: AMBER AGE - WORLD BIBLE (V3.0)

## 1. CORE CONCEPT (THE HYBRID REALITY)
*   **Balance:** 30% Modern History | 30% Elven Magic | 40% Interactive Romance.
*   **The Divergence (1974):** The moment when the Sky Breach opened, introducing Amber energy and activating dormant Elven lineages (Splits) in the human population.
*   **Aesthetic:** Elite Insider V2.6. High-tech modern world where ancient bloodlines rule through corporations and banks.

## 2. CORE MECHANICS & BIOLOGY
*   **The Elven Blood:** Splits are humans with activated elven genes. This grants them the Pulse (magical resonance) but causes the Amber Thirst.
*   **Sky Breach:** A jagged aurora-like rift. A gateway through which elven energy flows into our reality.
*   **Amber Edict (The Laws):** 
    1.  **Suppression Law:** All Elves/Splits must wear "Ethereal Suppressors" to prevent resonance leaks.
    2.  **White Sectors Protocol:** Restricted areas where magic-users cannot enter without a human "Anchor".
    3.  **The First Spark Edict:** Unauthorized deep resonance between two Elves is a crime against the Alliance.

## 3. ENVIRONMENTAL RESONANCE
*   **Realism Context:** Magic is physical. Empathy causes neurological stress. Resonance causes metallic frost or electrical interference (Static).
*   **The 1974 Forbidden Truth:** Amber was summoned by a ritual involving the Elven elders and human elite.

## 4. KEY LOCATIONS (RE-INTEGRATED)
*   **ELEJA (The Academic Hub):** Neoclassical palace for the Elven elite. High-tech labs studying the physics of blood and energy.
*   **RIGA-PRIME:** The vertical city of light. Power center of the Amber Alliance.
*   **KONIGSBERG:** The "Vatican of Alchemy". A cold, concrete monolith where ancient elven secrets are digitized.

## 5. SEASON 2: SHADOWS (REHEARSAL UPDATES)
*   **The Second Wave:** Mass awakening of Split resonance in 11th grade.
*   **Felix (Storm):** Incubus-destroyer from Lithuania. New primary antagonist/romance interest.
*   **Trust Bracelets:** Neurological monitoring of student emotions.
*   **Sub-Palace "Jupiter":** Forbidden research zone for the 1974 event.
