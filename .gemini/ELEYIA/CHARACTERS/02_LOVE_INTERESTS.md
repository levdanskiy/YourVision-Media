# 📂 ЛИЧНЫЕ ДЕЛА: ЗОЛОТАЯ ПЯТЕРКА

**9. МИКАС (Mikas)**
*   **Статус:** Сплит (Человек/Сатир), 19 лет.
*   **Роль:** Друг детства из Элеи, рабочий на янтарном заводе.
*   **Символ:** Лояльность и запах дома.
*   **Отношение к Яну:** Считает его своей «половиной». Простая, честная любовь.
*   **Секрет:** Боится, что если Ян уедет в Ригу, он навсегда потеряет его в неоновом шуме.
*   **Scent:** Sawn cedar, bitter espresso, and ozone before a storm.
*   **Aura:** Deep indigo, turning soft gold during intimacy. Feels like a heavy cashmere blanket.
*   **Touch:** Dry, hot, steady. Instantly "grounds" Jan's empathetic noise.
*   **Artifact:** «Тяжелый янтарный компас». Редкий прибор с каплей живого Дзинтарса вместо стрелки, который всегда указывает на Яна.
*   **Voice:** Warm crackling of firewood and the low hum of a cello.
*   **Shadow Hook:** Secret informant for the "Slavic Veche". Collecting data on Amber generator flaws in Eleja.
*   **Gestures:** Massages his knuckles when anxious. Never breaks eye contact, даже когда его Аура искрит золотом.
*   **Resonance:** Skin becomes noticeably hot; bracelet emits a low, calming hum.
*   **Intimacy Archetype:** «The Grounding Shelter» (Заземляющее убежище).
*   **Intimacy Behavior:** Primal, protective, and deeply physical. His lovemaking is heavy, slow, and focused on Jan's comfort.
*   **Amber Bleed:** His sweat smells of cedar; his Pulse turns Jan's skin warm gold.
*   **Taboo:** Physical Risk — His "Amber Thirst" is so vast he can accidentally drain Jan's Pulse to critical levels.
*   **Shadow Preference:** Validation Obsession. Needs Jan to scream his name to confirm his existence outside the factory.
*   **Obsession Trigger:** Jan's Submission. When the aristocrat-empath yields to the worker-split.
*   **Soul Sync:** Jan perceives his soul as an "ancient root" deep in Eleja's soil. Pure, unbreakable loyalty.
*   **Afterglow Anchor:** Mikas always covers Jan with his worker's jacket, whispering: "You're home now."
*   **Vulnerability Trigger:** His clumsy tenderness. When he tries to be delicate with his large, strong hands.
*   **Cinematic Setting:** Old attic in Eleja. Warm wood, smell of hay, vintage fishing nets, view of the forest under the amber Sky Breach.
*   **Cinematic Palette:** Ochre and honey gold. Soundtrack: Wind in the pines and distant factory hum.
*   **REVE Shots:** 1. Close-up: intertwined hands on rough linen under golden sunset. 2. Silhouettes by the window against a massive turquoise rift in the sky.

**10. ДОРИАН (Dorian)**

*   **Роль:** Интеллектуал из Кёнигсберга, «голос в телефоне».
*   **Символ:** Интеллект и идеальная мечта.
*   **Отношение к Яну:** Восхищается его эмпатией. Видит в нем спасение от скуки.
*   **Секрет:** Ведет двойную жизнь в Мана-Нет под псевдонимом «Shadow_Lord».
*   **Scent:** Old paper, cold mint, and expensive "Montblanc" ink.
*   **Aura:** Glass-transparent with geometric sparks. Feels like breathing arctic air.
*   **Touch:** Icy, nearly weightless. Touches the Pulse, not the skin.
*   **Artifact:** «Фрактальный монокль». Позволяет видеть «геометрию эмоций».
*   **Voice:** The rustle of thin ice needles and the chime of crystal.
*   **Shadow Hook:** Seeking a "key" to bypass Alliance protocols and restore Konigsberg.
*   **Gestures:** Adjusts AR-lenses to hide confusion. Fingers stop a millimeter from skin.
*   **Resonance:** Faint turquoise veins appear on his cheekbones ("Aesthetic Blush").
*   **Intimacy Archetype:** «The Fractal Architect» (Фрактальный архитектор).
*   **Intimacy Behavior:** Precise, experimental, and intellectualized. High-frequency vibrations.
*   **Amber Bleed:** Turquoise frost appears on his skin; the air becomes electric and cold.
*   **Taboo:** Mind Resonance — During Sync-3, thoughts blend; Jan might see state-secret codes.
*   **Shadow Preference:** Voyeurism. Prefers to "view" feelings through his monocle even during sex.
*   **Obsession Trigger:** Jan's Defiance. The intellectual challenge Jan presents before yielding.
*   **Soul Sync:** His soul is an "infinite library" under the aurora borealis. A vast sea of knowledge traded for a moment of presence.
*   **Afterglow Anchor:** Dorian draws an "infinity" fractal on Jan's palm, making it glow turquoise for a second.
*   **Vulnerability Trigger:** His admission of loneliness. When he turns off his AR-lenses and looks with "naked" eyes.
*   **Cinematic Setting:** Glass penthouse in Konigsberg. White marble, chrome, panoramic windows overlooking the Library of Alchemy.
*   **Cinematic Palette:** Arctic blue and sterile white. Soundtrack: Crystal silence with a faint electronic beat.
*   **REVE Shots:** 1. Reflections of faces in polished marble floor under neon turquoise light. 2. Bodies on white silk illuminated by the fractal monocle sparks.
