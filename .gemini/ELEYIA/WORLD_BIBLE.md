# 🪔 ELEYIA: AMBER AGE - WORLD BIBLE (V2.9)

## 1. CORE CONCEPT & BALANCE
*   **Balance:** 30% History | 30% Magic | 40% Romance.
*   **Divergence Point:** 1974 (The "Call" of Amber).

## 2. CORE MECHANICS & LAWS
*   **Sky Breach:** A jagged aurora-like rift since 1974. A gateway left slightly ajar.
*   **Amber Thirst:** Splits need physical "recharging" via Amber or life-force. 
*   **The Pulse:** Energy currency. Visible as a glow on ID-bracelets.
*   **Amber Edict (The Laws):** 
    1.  **Suppression Law:** Splits must wear "Ethereal Suppressors" in public.
    2.  **White Sectors Protocol:** Restricted areas for Splits after sunset.
    3.  **The First Spark Edict:** Remote pulse-locking for unauthorized resonance.
    4.  **The Echo Law:** Empaths are legally responsible for reflected emotions.
    5.  **Soul Biometrics Protocol:** Mandatory 30-day emotional audits.

## 3. ENVIRONMENTAL RESONANCE (JAN'S IMPACT)
*   **Anger:** Metallic surfaces frost over instantly (Cold Resonance).
*   **Fear:** AR-lens glitched with 1974 blueprints (Historical Ghosting).
*   **Trust:** Ambient lights pulse in sync with Jan's heartbeat (Golden Rhythm).

## 4. HIDDEN RESONANCE (JAN'S SECRETS)
*   **Memory Echo 1974:** Jan remembers the smell of sun-heated copper and the taste of ozone.
*   **The 1974 Forbidden Truth:** Amber was not discovered; it was summoned. The Sky Breach is a deliberate puncture in reality made by Jan's grandfather and the Directorate founders.

## 5. THE GLITCH ARCHIVE (AMBER ECHOES)
*   **Echo 1:** The sound of a thousand glass beads scattering on marble.
*   **Echo 2:** Visual of turquoise lightning trapped inside a soap bubble.
*   **Echo 3:** The scent of sun-heated copper a second before the Sky Breach.
*   **Echo 4:** The weight of a ghost-bird landing on a shoulder.
*   **Echo 5:** A split-second of total silence ending in a high 'B' note.

## 6. KEY LOCATIONS
*   **ELEJA (The Hub):** Neoclassical palace + botanical-tech labs. Ozone and magnolias.
*   **RIGA-PRIME:** Metropolis of Light. Crystal monoliths and MAGLEV trains.
*   **KONIGSBERG:** "Vatican of Alchemy". Ivory towers and amber-veined marble.

## 7. SEASONAL STRUCTURE (The 6-Year Saga)
*   **S1: Awakening:** Jan finds a spirit in Eleja Palace. Mikas romance.
*   **S2: Shadows:** Felix arrives. Darker mysteries in the city.
*   **S3: Choice:** Graduation. Sky Breach turns turquoise. Promise to Dorian.
*   **S4: Labyrinth:** Riga-Prime intro. Meeting Alex & Ray.
*   **S5: Masquerade:** European internship. Julian's world.
*   **S6: Finale:** Symphony. Global harmony. Return to Eleja.
